Track Banana account support

Request Account Deletion

Last updated: October 30, 2026

You can delete your Track Banana account in the app from your account or profile area by choosing Delete Account and confirming. You can also request deletion by emailing us from the address associated with your Track Banana account.

Email an account deletion request

What to include

Include the email address used for your Track Banana account and write “Track Banana account deletion request” in the message. Do not send your password. We may reply to verify that you control the account before processing the request.

What happens to your data

After an in-app deletion completes, your sign-in account is deleted, your profile name and username are replaced, your avatar link is removed, and personal expense entries are removed. Shared-house expense, meal, deposit, and settlement history may remain so other house members’ records and balances are preserved. An empty account with no other members is deleted.

For an email request, we will review the account and process the deletion request. If you need us to review particular information that remains in shared records, mention that in your request. See the Privacy Policy for more information about retention.
