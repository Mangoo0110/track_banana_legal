Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Aanda handles information when you use the Aanda mobile app for personal expenses, shared house expenses, meal tracking, and settlements. Aanda is provided by White Bun (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, Aanda handles:

  • Account information: email address and authentication information used to create and secure your account.
  • Profile information: username and, if you provide it, full name, profile photo, country, gender, and age range.
  • Expense and house information: house or account names, membership and roles, invite codes, currency and billing settings, expense amounts, categories, dates, notes, who paid, deposits, and settlement records.
  • Meal information: meal counts by member and date, which are used for shared-house calculations.
  • Technical information: our service providers may process connection, device, and diagnostic information needed to authenticate requests, operate the app, prevent abuse, and maintain service security.

How We Use Information

We use information to create and secure accounts; show your personal ledger; operate shared accounts and membership; record expenses, meals, deposits, and settlements; calculate balances; let members find or invite one another; provide profile images; respond to support requests; and maintain, protect, and improve the service.

Your personal expense ledger is intended to be visible only to you. Information entered in a shared house account, including expenses, meal counts, and settlements, is available to members of that account according to the app’s access controls and roles.

Storage and Service Providers

Aanda stores account and app data using Supabase services, including hosted authentication, database, and image storage. Supabase processes information on our behalf to provide these features. Profile and house photos that you upload are stored with the app’s storage provider and may be accessible to people who can view the relevant profile or house in the app.

We do not currently use advertising SDKs or third-party analytics in the app. If this changes, we will update this policy and the app’s store disclosures.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to provide the service: with Supabase as our hosting and authentication provider; with members of the shared account where you enter or contribute shared information; and where required to comply with law, protect users, or secure the service.

Retention and Account Deletion

You can request account deletion from the app’s account or profile area, or by following the instructions on our account deletion page. You may also contact dev.oniksaha@gmail.com.

When an in-app deletion request succeeds, the account’s authentication record is deleted, the profile name and username are replaced, and the avatar link is removed. Personal expenses are deleted. Shared-house expenses, meal records, and settlement history may be retained so the remaining members’ shared records and balances continue to work. If the user was the only member, the empty account is deleted. A user requesting deletion can ask us to review any remaining information by email.

We retain information only for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain shared account history, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and protect the service. Backups or provider logs may persist for a limited period under the provider’s normal retention and deletion processes.

Security

We use service-provider access controls and reasonable technical measures intended to protect information. No online service can guarantee complete security.

Children

Aanda is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly invite children under 13 to create accounts. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app or its data practices change. We will update the date at the top of this page when we make changes.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact White Bun at dev.oniksaha@gmail.com.
